# ADR-NNN: Short decision title

## Status

Proposed

## Context

Describe the problem, relevant constraints, and why a decision is needed now.
Separate observed facts from assumptions and intended future behavior.

## Decision

State the decision directly. Define ownership and boundaries where they matter,
without turning the ADR into an implementation transcript.

## Consequences

- Describe the important benefits.
- Describe the costs, constraints, and risks.
- Identify follow-up work without presenting it as already implemented.

## Alternatives considered

List credible alternatives and briefly explain why they were not selected.
Omit this section only when no meaningful alternative existed.

## Relations

Link ADRs that this decision supersedes, amends, depends on, or applies.
